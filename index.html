Questions

What businesses ask us before they hire a web designer.

How much does it cost to build a website for a small business?
It depends on what the website needs to do — a simple business website costs far less than an online store or a custom platform. Every project is quoted individually after a short conversation about your business, and you approve the full scope and price before any work begins. No hidden charges.
How long does it take to build a website?
A standard business website typically goes live within one to two weeks. Online stores, booking systems and custom platforms take longer depending on scope. Your timeline is confirmed in writing in your quote before we start.
Do you build e-commerce websites and online stores?
Yes. We build online stores with smooth, fast checkout, product and inventory management, and ordering options such as WhatsApp ordering — for retailers, grocery, fashion, electronics and more.
Can you build a client portal or custom web platform?
Yes — portals and platforms are our speciality. We built the web platform behind Rocket Estimation USA, a US construction estimating firm, including plan uploads, quote requests and client workflows. We also build dashboards, booking systems, learning platforms and internal business tools.
Will my website show up on Google?
Every build includes SEO architecture from day one: a site structure search engines reward, fast load times, and pages mapped to the searches your customers actually make. Several clients saw improved Google rankings within the first month of launch.
Do you work with businesses in the United States?
Yes. We work with clients in the US, Saudi Arabia and Pakistan, communicating by email, WhatsApp and video call. Our US work includes the Rocket Estimation USA platform, live in production and generating enquiries across North America.
